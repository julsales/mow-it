## Purpose


The daily and seasonal reasons to come back: a guided first minutes, daily login and quests, redeemable codes, achievement badges, and a monthly themed event layered over the everyday weather.

**Phase:** Phase 5 (v1.4) — guided first loop ships in Phase 1

## ADDED Requirements

### Requirement: Guided first loop
A new player's first minutes SHALL guide them through cutting, filling storage, selling and buying their first scissors with on-screen objectives and a reward at each step, before Crew and market systems are introduced.

#### Scenario: First-time player completes the loop
- **WHEN** a brand-new player follows the guided objectives
- **THEN** within about three minutes they have sold grass and bought their first tool upgrade, with each step paying a bonus

### Requirement: Seven-day daily login
Players SHALL receive an escalating daily login reward on a 7-day cycle, with the day-7 reward being a standout item such as a Crew member or a chunk of Clovers.

#### Scenario: Claiming day seven
- **WHEN** a player logs in on their seventh cycle day
- **THEN** they claim the cycle's headline reward and the cycle restarts

### Requirement: Daily and weekly quests
Players SHALL receive a small set of rotating daily quests plus weekly quests (for example cut, sell, contract and weather objectives) paying Coins, Clovers and Company experience.

#### Scenario: Completing a daily quest
- **WHEN** a player finishes a daily quest objective
- **THEN** they can claim its reward from the quest panel the same day

### Requirement: Redeemable codes
The game SHALL support redeemable codes that grant configured rewards once per account.

#### Scenario: Redeeming a code
- **WHEN** a player enters a valid unused code
- **THEN** the reward is granted and the same code cannot be redeemed again by that account

### Requirement: Achievement badges
Milestone achievements (cutting, selling, Crew, franchise and contract milestones) SHALL award badges shown on the player's showcase and profile.

#### Scenario: Earning a badge
- **WHEN** a player crosses a tracked milestone
- **THEN** the badge is awarded with a celebration and appears on their showcase

### Requirement: Monthly themed event
Roughly monthly, a themed event of about two weeks SHALL add event Crew to the restock pool, event decorations and a temporary event currency that is only used inside that event.

#### Scenario: Playing during an event
- **WHEN** an event is active and a player opens the Crew market
- **THEN** the event's limited Crew can appear in the restock shelf, purchasable as the event defines
