## Art direction

- Saturated simulator colors, dimensional toy-like objects, strong readable
  silhouettes, painted shading, bevels and controlled specular highlights.
- Slim navy/colored edges, approximately 1–2 screen pixels at a 64px icon.
  Remove the previous uniform thick brown strokes and block shadows.
- Panels: layered edge, recessed dark teal body, cyan header treatment,
  subtle surface pattern and soft offset shadow. No ornamental medieval trim.
- Pass banners: rich gold, green, blue and coral variants; large specific
  illustrations; restrained radial rays, diagonal light sweeps and sparkles.
- All live text stays native Roblox text. Generate text-free skins and
  illustrations separately; preserve price/title/description/button spaces.
- Keep effect layers, objects, button surfaces and window skins independent.
  A single flattened screenshot cannot replace the interactive interface.

### 1. Concept — `mow-it-simulator-concept-v3.png`

Use case: ui-mockup. Create a high-fidelity game interface concept for Mow It!,
a Roblox mowing simulator. Image 1 is the owner's primary simulator UI style
and layout reference; Image 2 is only a supporting reference for farming icon
subjects. Follow Image 1's visual richness: glossy beveled square navigation,
saturated colors, large shaded collectible icons, subtle surface textures,
layered panel edges, diagonal highlights and illustrated offer banners with
radial light. Use substantially slimmer outlines than the rejected flat
board: crisp dark-navy edges, no heavy brown cartoon contours. Keep the world
behind the HUD softly visible. Left navigation: Crew, Shop, Quests, Rewards;
Coins and Clovers prominent directly underneath. Right: distinct weather and
fuel indicators, vertically stacked. Center: scrollable passes window with
an obvious close button, an illustrated gold 2x Coins offer and distinct
Crew/Auto-Sell/Fuel offers. The window title is PASSES. Offer purchase buttons
read COMING SOON, without invented prices, urgency or discount claims.
English only. Use readable bold white lettering with a restrained dark edge.
Keep generous separation, clear interaction targets and no clipping. Avoid
flat placeholder symbols, identical blank rectangles, heavy rounded brown
frames, generic minimal dashboards, copied reference characters or logos.
